Privacy Policy

  • SIM DAD LLC

This Privacy Policy describes how SIM DAD LLC (“SIM DAD,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Lector desktop application and the Lector website at uselector.app. Lector is a local Markdown editor with text-to-speech that runs entirely on your machine.

1. Information the Application Collects

Lector processes all documents and audio entirely on your local machine. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your documents, text content, audio output, voice profiles, or usage data.

The only information transmitted from the application is during license key activation, which includes:

  • Your email address (as provided during purchase)
  • A one-way hash derived from your machine, used for advisory seat counting. It is not reversible, and your hostname, username and hardware addresses are not transmitted.

This activation data is processed by SIM DAD LLC’s own licensing service. No third party receives it, and no other data leaves your machine through the application.

2. Information the Website Collects

The Lector website at uselector.app may collect the following information:

  • Email address. If you voluntarily subscribe to our waitlist or mailing list, your email address is received via a Cloudflare Worker form endpoint and stored in our Google Workspace inbox. We do not currently use a third-party email service provider; if that changes, this policy will be updated and existing subscribers will be notified.
  • Analytics data. We do not use third-party analytics, and this site sets no analytics cookies. Each page sends a single, first-party, cookie-free page-view count to an endpoint we operate; it records only the page name and the date, is skipped entirely when your browser sends Do Not Track, and stores no IP address and no identifier.

3. No Telemetry or Cloud Processing

Lector does not include any telemetry or usage analytics. All text editing, Markdown rendering, and text-to-speech processing happens 100% locally on your device. No document content or audio data is ever sent to SIM DAD LLC or any third party. The only data the developer can ever receive is a crash report that you choose to send, described below.

Error reports

Lector does not transmit any data automatically. If Lector encounters an unexpected error, you may be prompted to send a crash report. Crash reports are sent only when you click Send, are addressed to support+lector@simdadllc.com through your own mail client, and contain only the following information:

  • the product name and version
  • the date and time of the crash
  • the technical error (traceback)
  • your operating system and version
  • your CPU model and total RAM
  • (if relevant to the crash) your Python runtime version

Crash reports never contain documents, audio, voice profiles, file paths from your home directory, environment variables, your username, your hostname, your machine identifier, your IP address, your license key, or your order information. File paths inside the technical error are automatically redacted before the report is shown to you. There is no third-party error-tracking service.

You can disable crash-report prompts at any time in Settings → Privacy. You can review or delete saved reports in the same panel.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Gumroad. Payment processing only. Gumroad handles your purchase transaction. It does not issue, manage or validate license keys, which are operated entirely by SIM DAD LLC. Their privacy policy is available at gumroad.com/privacy.
  • Cloudflare. Form-handling Worker for waitlist and contact form submissions. The Worker receives your email address and forwards it to our Google Workspace inbox; Cloudflare does not retain submission contents beyond the time needed to forward them. Their privacy policy is available at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • Google Workspace. Storage of waitlist and contact form submissions in our Gmail inbox. Their privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

License activation data is retained for as long as your license is active. Email addresses collected through the mailing list are retained until you unsubscribe or request removal.

To request deletion of your personal data, contact us at support@simdadllc.com. We will process your request within 30 days.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited personal data we collect. Because Lector processes all content locally, your documents and audio never leave your device and are protected by your own system’s security.

7. Children’s Privacy

Lector is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us at support@simdadllc.com and we will promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of Lector after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
