How it works

Write. Pause.
Let it read back to you.

Lector plays your writing back to you at the sentence level, with word-by-word highlighting, so you catch what your eyes skip over. Every voice — including your own — runs on your machine.

1

Open or paste your draft

Paste text directly, or open a .docx or .md file. Lector strips citation markers automatically so your text reads cleanly.

2

Click anywhere and press Play

Lector starts reading from wherever your cursor is. Each word is highlighted as it's spoken. Press Escape to stop at any point.

3

Hear it in any voice

Choose from 10 built-in Edge TTS neural voices, or clone any voice from a 10–20 second audio sample. Cloned voices use F5-TTS locally — no account, no upload.

4

Save your draft

Auto-saves your text in the browser between sessions. Export as .md any time with Ctrl+S or the Save button.

Why Lector?

Your ears catch what
your eyes miss.

Reading your own writing silently is unreliable — your brain fills in what it expects, not what's on the page. Hearing it read back forces a different kind of attention. Lector makes that part of the workflow, not an afterthought.

And because every voice runs locally, your manuscript never touches a server. Sensitive work stays sensitive.

System requirements

Runs on everyday hardware.

Built-in voices work on any modern Windows PC. Voice cloning requires an NVIDIA GPU — but it’s optional, not required.

Operating System

Windows 10 or 11

macOS / Linux supported via launch.sh

Python

3.12

Installed automatically on first launch via setup script

GPU (optional)

NVIDIA with CUDA 12.4

Required for voice cloning only. Built-in voices work on CPU.

First-run download

~3 GB

PyTorch and F5-TTS model weights. Downloaded once, then offline.
