The problem

You read what you meant to write.

Every writer knows the feeling. You read a paragraph three times and it looks fine. Then you hear it read aloud and immediately catch the sentence that runs too long, the word that appears twice in four lines, the transition that doesn’t quite land.

That’s not a grammar problem. It’s a prose problem. And the eye misses it because the brain already knows what you meant to say.

Hearing your writing is different. The voice doesn’t know what you intended. It reads what’s actually there.

The solution

Write here. Hear it here.

Lector puts the read-aloud function inside the writing environment itself. No copying text into another application, no browser tabs, no switching tools mid-edit. You write, you listen, you revise — all in the same place.

Word-by-word highlighting follows the voice so you always know exactly where you are. Click anywhere to start from that point. Jump back a sentence. Change the reading speed. Use one of Lector’s built-in voices, or spend a few minutes building your own.

What Lector does

Everything you need to hear your work.

Read aloud with word-by-word highlighting

Follow every word as Lector reads. Click anywhere to jump to that point. No separate app required — the read-aloud is built into the same surface where you write.

A writing environment that gets out of the way

Distraction-free by design. WYSIWYG mode renders formatting as you type. Raw Markdown available for those who want it. Toggle between them without losing your place.

Your voice, built once

Record thirty seconds of audio. Lector builds a voice profile from it. After that, every read-aloud session uses your own voice — or a voice familiar to you. Built once, yours permanently.

Completely local. Nothing leaves your machine.

No cloud processing. No account required. No internet connection needed after setup. Your drafts and your voice stay on your computer, under your control.
